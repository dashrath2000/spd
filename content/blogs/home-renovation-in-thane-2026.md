## 4. Bathroom Remodeling & 10-Year Polymer Waterproofing
Bathroom leakage is the single most common cause of inter-flat disputes and wall dampness in high-rise societies across Thane. Proper waterproofing during bathroom renovation is critical.

### The SPD 5-Layer Waterproofing Process
1. Complete removal of old floor tiles and screed down to the concrete slab base.
2. Pressure washing and sealing floor crack joints with non-shrink polymer grout.
3. Application of 2 coats of flexible elastomeric cementitious slurry waterproofing membrane.
4. Water ponding testing for 72 consecutive hours to check zero seepage.
5. Laying anti-skid 600x600mm vitrified tiles over high-bond tile adhesive.

## 5. Navigating Society NOC & Municipal Approvals
Before initiating any noisy civil work, demolition, or tile chipping in Thane, homeowners must secure official approval from their Housing Co-operative Society (CHS) or municipal bodies.

### Required Documents for Society NOC
- Written Renovation Application detailing structural scope.
- Architect / Contractor Structural Undertaking (confirming no load-bearing columns or beams will be damaged).
- List of certified workers with Govt photo ID verification.
- Security Deposit payment (typically refundable ₹10,000 to ₹50,000).

## 8. Flooring Upgrades: Vitrified Tiles vs. Italian Marble
Flooring forms the acoustic and visual foundation of your home.

- **Double-Charged Vitrified Tiles (800x1600mm):** Extremely durable, scratch-resistant, stain-proof, and budget-friendly.
- **Italian Marble (Dyna / Botticino):** Offers timeless luxury with unique vein patterns; requires diamond polishing and protective resin coats.
- **Wooden Laminate Flooring:** Ideal for master bedrooms to provide cozy warmth underfoot.
